# IPTV: Xtream and M3U providers

Status: approved scope, phase 1 in progress (2026-10-31). Phase 3 (films and series) done on branch `iptv-vod`.

## Goal

A viewer adds an IPTV provider (Xtream Codes login, or an M3U playlist URL with an optional
XMLTV guide URL) and gets a Live TV area that is quick to use with a remote and with touch:
channel groups, favourites, search, a guide, the provider's films and series, and catch-up.
The providers live in the app, not on the Jellyfin server.

## Decisions

- **In the app, not through Jellyfin.** Jellyfin Live TV has no Xtream support and needs
  server setup. A provider belongs to the device and the signed-in account (like the query
  cache: keyed by server Id and user Id), so two accounts never share providers.
- **Credentials** (Xtream password, M3U URLs that hold a token) go to the secure store, never
  into MMKV or logs. Provider records in MMKV are versioned with a migration, like download
  records.
- **Big data goes to files, not MMKV.** An M3U list can hold 50 000 entries and an XMLTV file
  can be 100 MB. Parse in chunks that yield to the event loop, keep the result in
  `<documents>/iptv/<providerId>/` (channels as JSON, guide as a compact window), and keep
  only small indexes in memory. Low-memory TV sticks are the target.
- **One import surface:** `@/iptv` (hooks and an `iptv` object), like `@/downloads`. Screens
  never call a provider API directly. Pure parsers and rules in their own files with tests.
- **Playback goes through the existing player** (`usePlayer`, `videoSession`), with a new play
  request kind for a stream URL (no Jellyfin item, no progress report to Jellyfin). Live
  streams have no duration; the scrubber hides for live, and shows for VOD and catch-up.
- **Stream format per platform.** Xtream live offers `.m3u8` and `.ts`; ask for HLS on iOS and
  web, TS on Android and the Mac app (ExoPlayer and libmpv read both). An M3U entry with a
  plain `.ts` URL does not play on iOS (AVPlayer has no progressive MPEG-TS): the channel shows
  "This channel's format does not play on iPhone and iPad" instead of failing silently.
- **Web and the Mac app.** Most providers send no CORS headers. The Mac app fetches lists and
  guides through a Rust command (reqwest is already a dependency). The plain web build tries a
  direct fetch and, when CORS blocks it, says that IPTV needs the app or a provider that
  allows it. `src/platform/tv.ts` `features` gets an `iptv` flag per platform.
- **TV first.** Every screen works with the D-pad: focus enters the page, Left from the first
  column opens the side menu, channel up and down zap in the player, Back leaves the player
  to the channel it played. Favourites with a long press (touch) or the menu key / a button on
  the focused row (TV).

## Phases

Each phase is its own branch, reviewed and merged before the next one.

1. **Providers and live channels.** Add, edit and remove providers (Xtream: server URL, user
   name, password; M3U: playlist URL, optional XMLTV URL, optional user agent). Load and cache
   the channel list with a refresh. A "Live TV" destination (tab on phone, side menu on TV) with
   channel groups, a Favourites group first, Recently watched, and search across channel names.
   Favourite and unfavourite a channel. Play a channel in the player; channel up/down and a
   channel list overlay in the player. Now and next for each channel where the provider has a
   guide (Xtream `get_short_epg`, or the XMLTV file).
2. **Guide.** A grid guide (channels down, time across), D-pad and touch scrolling, jump to
   now, open a programme for details, play the channel. Full XMLTV parse into a rolling window.
3. **Films and series.** Xtream VOD and series as browsable libraries in the Live TV area:
   categories, details, seasons and episodes, resume positions kept on the device.
   **Done (branch `iptv-vod`).** Also: the film and episode entries of M3U lists (`/movie/`,
   `/series/` or a video file type; episodes grouped by "S01 E02" names), title search, favourites,
   Continue watching on the Films and Series pages, next episode through the next-up card. MKV on
   iOS: the panel's `.m3u8` form, else a message.
4. **Catch-up.** Programmes in the past on channels with an archive (`tv_archive`,
   `tv_archive_duration`) play from the guide through the Xtream timeshift URL.

## Not in scope

Recording, multi-view, parental PINs, provider-side favourites sync, and sending IPTV
playback to Jellyfin.
