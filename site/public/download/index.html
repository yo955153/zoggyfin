Zoggyfin for Mac

Download for Mac

The newest version, as a signed and notarized disk image.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

  1. Open Zoggyfin-arm64.dmg.
  2. Drag Zoggyfin to the Applications folder.
  3. Open Zoggyfin from Applications or Launchpad.

Zoggyfin looks for updates by itself. When a new version is available, it asks you before it installs it. To look now, select Check for Updates… in the Zoggyfin menu.
