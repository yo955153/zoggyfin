Mac

Zoggyfin for Mac

The newest version, as a signed and notarized disk image.

Download for Mac

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

  1. Open Zoggyfin-arm64.dmg.
  2. Drag Zoggyfin to the Applications folder.
  3. Open Zoggyfin from Applications or Launchpad.

Zoggyfin looks for updates by itself and asks before it installs one. To look now, select Check for Updates… in the Zoggyfin menu.

What is new

    Fire TV and Google TV

    Zoggyfin for TV

    The newest version, as an APK for Fire TV, Google TV and Android TV.

    Download the APKGet it on Google Play

    On the TV, type this short address: zoggyfin.zog-tech.com/tv.apk. Needs Android TV 7 or later, or Fire OS 6 or later.

    Zoggyfin looks for updates by itself. The first update asks you to let Zoggyfin install apps.

    Fire TV

    1. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore.
    2. If you do not see Developer options, go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and select the device name 7 times.
    3. In Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps, turn on Downloader.
    4. Open Downloader, type zoggyfin.zog-tech.com/tv.apk and select Install.

    Google TV and Android TV

    1. Install Downloader from Google Play on the TV.
    2. In Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources, turn on Downloader.
    3. Open Downloader, type zoggyfin.zog-tech.com/tv.apk and select Install.

    With a computer on the same network, you can also use ADB: adb install zoggyfin-tv-latest.apk.
