Library

Artwork on every tile.

Posters, album covers and episode stills load fast and stay sharp. When your server has no image for an item, Zoggyfin draws one from its title. You never see an empty grey box.

  • Home rows for Continue watching, Next up and Recently added.
  • Music by albums, artists, playlists, songs and genres, sorted the way you like.
  • Search that groups results into movies, shows, episodes, music and people as you type.

Video

A player that just plays.

Files play directly when your device can open them, so the server only converts what it must. On Mac, the libmpv engine plays MKV, HEVC, AV1, DTS and TrueHD with no conversion.

  • Direct play
  • Picture in Picture
  • AirPlay
  • Subtitles
  • Skip Intro
  • Chapters
  • Quality control
  • Background audio

Music and audio

Music that keeps playing.

A full music player with synced lyrics, a queue you can reorder, background audio and lock-screen controls.

  • Audiobooks, lectures and podcasts get a long-form player: playback speed, skip 15 or 30 seconds, and a sleep timer.
  • Every long listen resumes where you stopped, on each item.
  • Media keys and the lock screen control playback on every device.

Downloads

Downloads you can see.

Every download shows its progress, its size and the space left on your device. Get a movie, an episode, a whole season, an album or a playlist. It plays with no connection, and your progress goes back to the server later.

Together

Watch and listen together.

Tap Together on Home, then join a session or start one. Everyone stays in step: play, pause and seek for all.

People near you

On a plane, a train or a cabin with no signal. One device hosts and the others join over the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Works between iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac. You accept each person, and you can keep the session to people with your code.

Anyone on your server

Start a session on your Jellyfin server and watch with friends in other places. It uses Jellyfin SyncPlay, so people on other Jellyfin apps can join too.

iPad and Mac

Made for every screen.

On iPad and Mac, Zoggyfin uses the room: a sidebar, wider grids and a bigger player. On iPhone, everything is one thumb away.

  • Keyboard shortcuts in the player: Space, arrow keys, subtitles and full screen.
  • Media keys and Now Playing on Mac, with updates that install when you say so.
  • Pick up where you stopped on any device. Your progress lives on your server.

Your servers

Home, away and everyone in between.

Add as many servers as you like. Zoggyfin finds the address that works and helps you share your server with the people you choose.

Home and internet addresses

Give a server more than one address. The app uses the first one that answers, or the home address on your home Wi-Fi.

Tailscale and VPN

Addresses on Tailscale or another VPN work like any other. Invites tell the other person to connect first.

Invites by text

Add a person and send one link by message or QR code. They tap it, Zoggyfin signs them in, and they set their own password.

Admin panel

For server admins: users, devices, active sessions, libraries, scheduled tasks and logs, right in the app.

Playlists in two taps

Tap Playlist on any movie, episode or song, then pick a playlist or make a new one.

Continue on any device

Pause on your iPhone and resume on your Mac. Progress syncs through your server.

Quick Connect sign-in

Sign in with a code from another device instead of a password.

Precise, always

Exact sizes, speeds and times everywhere. No vague spinners.

Extensions

Make it yours.

An extension adds features for your own setup, with no change to the app. A self-hosted tool can add buttons on items, rows on Home, a share target or a whole page.

  • Data only. The app reads a JSON manifest from your server. No code from an extension runs in the app.
  • You decide. A confirm screen shows each permission before you add it. Turn it off or remove it at any time.

    "itemActions": […],

    "shareTargets": […]
  }

Privacy

It talks only to your server.

Zoggyfin has no account of its own and collects no data. Your media, your watch history and your settings stay between your devices and your server.

  • No analytics and no tracking
  • No ads and no account to make
  • Sign-in tokens kept in the device keychain
  • Nothing goes through our servers

Questions

Something else? Write to admin@zog-tech.com.

Do I need a Jellyfin server?

Yes. Zoggyfin is a client: it plays the media on a Jellyfin server that you or someone you trust runs. You need Jellyfin 10.11 or later and a user account on it.

To try the app first, use the public demo server: https://demo.jellyfin.org/stable, user name demo, no password.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Does Zoggyfin collect any data?

No. The app talks only to the servers you add. We have no account system and no analytics. The privacy policy has the details.

Will my files play without conversion?

Most will. Zoggyfin asks the server for direct play when your device can open the file, and the server converts only what it must. On Mac, the libmpv engine also plays MKV, HEVC, AV1, DTS and TrueHD directly.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes. Give your server a home address and an internet, Tailscale or VPN address. Zoggyfin uses the one that answers. Downloads play with no connection at all.

When does Android come?

Soon. Zoggyfin for Android phones and tablets is in testing now. The download page links to it when it is ready.

Is it open source?

Soon. We are preparing the code for a public release under the MPL-2.0.

Is Zoggyfin made by the Jellyfin project?

No. Zoggyfin is made by Zog Tech, Inc. and is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project. Jellyfin is the free server software that Zoggyfin connects to.
