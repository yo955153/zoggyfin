Your media server, in your pocket.

Zoggyfin is a fast, calm client for your own Jellyfin media server on iPhone and iPad.

  • Movies, shows and music from your server, with artwork on every tile.
  • A native video player with Picture in Picture, AirPlay, subtitles and Skip Intro.
  • A music player with lyrics, a queue, background audio and lock-screen controls.
  • Downloads you can see: progress, size and what is on the device.
  • Watch together with SyncPlay, or with a nearby device on the same network.

You need a Jellyfin server to use Zoggyfin. Zoggyfin has no account of its own, no ads and no tracking.
